# Redis Eats Delivery Delay Policy

## Overview

Redis Eats works hard to deliver food on time. This policy explains what constitutes a delivery delay, how Redis Eats responds to delays, and what compensation customers may receive.

---

## Estimated Delivery Time

When you place an order, Redis Eats provides an **Estimated Delivery Time (EDT)** based on:

- Restaurant preparation time
- Current driver availability
- Distance between the restaurant and your address
- Real-time traffic conditions

The EDT is an estimate, not a guarantee. Minor variations of 5–10 minutes are normal and expected.

---

## What Counts as a Delay

A delivery is considered delayed when it arrives more than **15 minutes past the EDT** shown at order placement. Redis Eats tracks this automatically using driver GPS data.

---

## What Happens When Your Order Is Delayed

Redis Eats monitors all active deliveries in real time. When a significant delay is detected:

1. **You will receive a push notification** updating your estimated delivery time.
2. **The app will show live tracking** for your driver's current location.
3. **Support is automatically alerted** for orders delayed more than 30 minutes.

---

## Compensation for Delays

| Delay Duration | Compensation |
|---|---|
| Under 15 minutes past EDT | No compensation — within normal range |
| 15–30 minutes past EDT | Redis Eats Credit applied to your account |
| 30–45 minutes past EDT | Larger credit or partial refund on delivery fee |
| Over 45 minutes past EDT | Eligible for full refund — see Refund Policy |

Compensation is applied automatically for orders monitored by the system. If you believe you experienced a delay that was not compensated, contact Redis Eats Support within 48 hours.

---

## Common Causes of Delays

- High order volume during peak hours (lunch 11 AM–1 PM, dinner 6–8 PM)
- Restaurant preparation taking longer than estimated
- Traffic, road closures, or adverse weather
- Driver availability in your area

---

## Weather and Extraordinary Events

During severe weather or other extraordinary events (major sporting events, holidays), delivery times may be significantly longer than normal. Redis Eats will display a warning banner on the app during these periods. Delays caused by weather or extraordinary events may not be eligible for compensation under standard policy, but Redis Eats Support can review requests individually.

---

## How to Report a Delay

If your order is significantly delayed and you have not received automatic communication:

1. Open the Redis Eats app.
2. Go to **Active Orders** and select your order.
3. Tap **Get Help** or **Report a Problem**.
4. Select **Order is taking too long**.

Support will investigate and apply any applicable compensation.

---

## Contact Support

For delay-related questions, contact Redis Eats Support through the app or at support@rediseats.example.com.
