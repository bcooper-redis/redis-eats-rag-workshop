# Redis Eats Order Status FAQ

## Overview

This document answers the most common questions customers have about order status, tracking, and what to do when something seems wrong with their order.

---

## What Do the Order Statuses Mean?

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Order Placed** | Your order has been submitted. Redis Eats is waiting for the restaurant to confirm. |
| **Order Confirmed** | The restaurant has accepted your order and is preparing it. |
| **Being Prepared** | The restaurant is actively preparing your food. |
| **Driver Assigned** | A Redis Eats driver has been matched to your order and is heading to the restaurant. |
| **Driver at Restaurant** | Your driver has arrived at the restaurant and is waiting for your food. |
| **Order Picked Up** | Your driver has collected your food and is heading to you. |
| **Arriving Soon** | Your driver is nearby. Estimated arrival is less than 5 minutes. |
| **Delivered** | Your order has been marked as delivered. |

---

## How Do I Track My Order?

Once a driver has been assigned to your order, you can track their real-time location in the Redis Eats app:

1. Open the app.
2. Go to **Active Orders** or tap the banner at the top of the home screen.
3. A live map will show your driver's current location and estimated arrival time.

Tracking is available from the moment a driver is assigned until the order is marked delivered.

---

## My Order Is Stuck on "Order Placed" — What Should I Do?

If your order has been in the "Order Placed" state for more than **10 minutes**, the restaurant may be slow to confirm. Redis Eats automatically follows up with the restaurant after this time.

If you are concerned:

1. Go to your active order in the app.
2. Tap **Get Help**.
3. Select **Order not confirmed**.

Support will investigate and keep you updated.

---

## My Order Was Marked Delivered but I Did Not Receive It

If your order shows as delivered but you do not have it:

1. Check around your delivery location (door, lobby, etc.).
2. Check if a neighbor may have accidentally received it.
3. If still not found, open the app, go to **Order History**, and select the order.
4. Tap **Report a Problem** and choose **Order says delivered but I didn't receive it**.

Redis Eats will investigate using driver GPS data and delivery photos. If the delivery cannot be confirmed, you may be eligible for a full refund. See the Refund Policy for details.

---

## Can I Change My Delivery Address After Placing an Order?

Address changes after order placement are limited. If the order is still in **Order Placed** status:

1. Contact Redis Eats Support immediately through the app.
2. An agent may be able to update the address if the driver has not yet been assigned.

Once a driver has been assigned, the delivery address cannot be changed.

---

## Can I Add Items to My Order After It Has Been Placed?

No. Once an order is submitted, items cannot be added. You would need to place a separate order. If your first order is still being prepared, the two deliveries may arrive close together.

---

## Why Did My Estimated Delivery Time Change?

Estimated delivery times may change due to:

- The restaurant taking longer to prepare your food than originally estimated.
- Driver reassignment (if the original driver had an issue).
- Traffic or road conditions changing.

The app will notify you when your estimated delivery time changes significantly.

---

## My Order Was Canceled — What Happens Now?

If your order was canceled (by you, the restaurant, or Redis Eats), you will receive a notification with the reason. Depending on the cancellation type:

- If canceled before preparation: full refund.
- If canceled during preparation: partial refund may apply.
- If canceled due to no driver availability: full refund.

See the Cancellation Policy for full details.
