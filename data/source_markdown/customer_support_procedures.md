# Redis Eats Customer Support Procedures

## Overview

This document describes how the Redis Eats customer support team operates, what support channels are available, and what customers can expect when they contact support.

---

## Support Channels

Redis Eats offers three main support channels:

| Channel | Best For | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **In-App Chat** | Order issues, refunds, account help | 24/7 |
| **Email** | Non-urgent questions, documentation requests | 24/7 (response within 24 hours) |
| **Phone** | Complex or urgent issues | 8 AM – 10 PM local time |

For the fastest response, use **In-App Chat**. This connects you directly to a support agent with access to your order details.

---

## Contacting Support Through the App

1. Open the Redis Eats app.
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Help**.
4. Browse help topics or tap **Contact Us** to start a chat.

For issues related to a specific order, go to **Order History**, select the order, and tap **Get Help** or **Report a Problem**. This routes your request to an agent who already has your order details.

---

## What to Have Ready When Contacting Support

To help resolve your issue quickly, have the following ready:

- Your account email address
- Your order number (found in **Order History**)
- A brief description of the issue
- Any photos that might be relevant (e.g., wrong items, damaged food)

---

## Response Time Expectations

| Channel | Expected Response Time |
|---|---|
| In-App Chat | Under 5 minutes during peak hours; typically immediate |
| Email | Within 24 hours |
| Phone | Under 10 minutes hold time during business hours |

Response times may be longer during high-demand periods such as major holidays or severe weather events.

---

## Issue Resolution Process

When you contact support:

1. **Triage:** The agent reviews your account and order history.
2. **Investigation:** For complex issues (e.g., missing items, delivery disputes), the agent may review GPS data, driver reports, or restaurant confirmations.
3. **Resolution:** The agent will offer one or more of the following:
   - Refund (to original payment or Redis Eats Credit)
   - Replacement order
   - Account credit
   - Escalation to a specialist team
4. **Confirmation:** You will receive a confirmation email summarizing the resolution.

Most issues are resolved within the same support session.

---

## Escalations

If you are not satisfied with the initial resolution:

1. Ask the agent to escalate your case to a supervisor or specialist team.
2. Escalated cases are reviewed within **2 business days**.
3. You will be contacted via email with the outcome of the escalation.

---

## Fraud and Unauthorized Account Activity

If you suspect unauthorized activity on your account (unrecognized orders, changed contact information):

1. Contact Redis Eats Support immediately through **In-App Chat** or by phone.
2. An agent will secure your account, review recent activity, and initiate a fraud investigation.
3. Change your password as soon as possible through **Account Settings > Security**.

Do not share your account credentials with anyone.

---

## Feedback and Complaints

Redis Eats values customer feedback. To submit general feedback or a formal complaint:

1. Go to **Help > Give Feedback** in the app.
2. Or email feedback@rediseats.example.com.

Formal complaints are reviewed by our Customer Experience team and may result in process improvements.
