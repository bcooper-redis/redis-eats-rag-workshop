# Redis Eats Food Safety Policy

## Overview

Redis Eats is committed to the safety of every meal delivered through our platform. This policy describes the food safety standards we expect from restaurant partners and drivers, and what customers can do if they have a food safety concern.

---

## Restaurant Partner Food Safety Requirements

All restaurant partners on Redis Eats must:

- Hold a valid food service license issued by their local health authority.
- Pass a food safety review as part of the onboarding process.
- Maintain a passing health inspection score. Redis Eats reserves the right to suspend partners who receive failing inspection scores.
- Follow proper food handling, storage, and preparation procedures at all times.
- Package food securely to prevent contamination or spillage during delivery.

Redis Eats conducts periodic reviews of partner health inspection records. Partners with repeated health violations may be removed from the platform.

---

## Packaging Standards

Restaurants are expected to package orders in a way that:

- Keeps hot food hot and cold food cold during transit.
- Prevents leaks and spills.
- Seals containers to reduce tampering risk.
- Separates raw and cooked items where applicable.
- Includes tamper-evident seals where possible.

Redis Eats provides packaging guidance to new restaurant partners during onboarding.

---

## Driver Food Handling Requirements

Delivery drivers are responsible for handling food safely during transit. Drivers must:

- Use insulated delivery bags provided or approved by Redis Eats.
- Keep food upright and stable during transport.
- Not open, tamper with, or consume any part of a customer's order.
- Report damaged or compromised packaging to the restaurant before pickup when possible.

Drivers who violate food handling requirements may have their accounts suspended.

---

## Temperature-Sensitive Orders

Certain orders (such as raw seafood, dairy products, or ice cream) require temperature control throughout delivery. Restaurant partners offering these items must use appropriate cold packaging. Drivers must use insulated cold bags for these deliveries.

If a customer receives temperature-sensitive food that appears to have been stored incorrectly (e.g., ice cream fully melted, seafood at room temperature), they should report the issue through the app immediately.

---

## Reporting a Food Safety Concern

If you believe a food safety issue has occurred:

1. Stop consuming the food immediately.
2. Open the Redis Eats app and go to **Order History**.
3. Select the affected order and tap **Report a Problem**.
4. Choose **Food Safety Concern** from the issue list.
5. Describe the issue and attach photos if possible.

Redis Eats takes food safety reports seriously. Reported concerns are forwarded to the relevant restaurant partner and reviewed by our Trust and Safety team.

If you believe the issue poses an immediate health risk, contact your local health authority.

---

## Allergen Responsibility

Redis Eats provides allergen information based on data supplied by restaurant partners. While we make every effort to keep this information accurate:

- Customers with severe allergies should contact the restaurant directly to confirm ingredients before ordering.
- Redis Eats cannot guarantee that menu items are free of cross-contamination.
- Restaurant partners are responsible for the accuracy of allergen information on their menus.

---

## Contact Food Safety Support

For food safety concerns, contact Redis Eats Support through the app or at safety@rediseats.example.com.
