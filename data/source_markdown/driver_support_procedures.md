# Redis Eats Driver Support Procedures

## Overview

This document describes support procedures for Redis Eats delivery drivers (Dashers). It covers how drivers can get help, report issues, and navigate common situations that arise during deliveries.

---

## Getting Started as a Driver

Before your first delivery, ensure you have completed:

- Identity verification through the Redis Eats Driver app
- Vehicle and insurance documentation upload
- Background check clearance
- Driver orientation (available in the Driver app under **Getting Started**)

If any step is incomplete, your account will be on hold until the requirement is fulfilled. Contact Driver Support for assistance.

---

## Reporting a Problem During a Delivery

If you encounter an issue while on an active delivery, use the Redis Eats Driver app:

1. Open the Driver app and tap your active delivery.
2. Tap **Help** in the top right corner.
3. Select the issue type:
   - Restaurant not ready / long wait
   - Cannot find the restaurant
   - Customer not responding
   - Cannot find the delivery address
   - Food was damaged
   - Accident or safety concern

For accidents or safety concerns, call emergency services first, then report through the app.

---

## Restaurant Wait Times

If a restaurant is not ready when you arrive:

- Wait up to **10 minutes** before reporting a delay.
- After 10 minutes, tap **Report Restaurant Delay** in the app.
- Redis Eats will contact the restaurant and may reassign the order if the delay is excessive.

Waiting time beyond 10 minutes may be eligible for additional pay. See the Compensation section in your Driver Agreement.

---

## Customer Not Available

If a customer does not respond at delivery:

1. Attempt to contact the customer through the in-app messaging feature.
2. Wait at least **5 minutes** after arrival before leaving.
3. If the customer is still unreachable, take a photo of the food left at the door (if safe to do so) and mark the order as **Delivered — Left at Door**.
4. Customers who request "leave at door" in their delivery preferences should be treated this way by default.

Do not leave food if the location appears unsafe or if leaving food could create a hazard.

---

## Handling Damaged or Incorrect Food

If you notice that food is damaged or incorrect before leaving the restaurant:

- Notify the restaurant staff immediately.
- If the restaurant can correct the issue quickly (under 5 minutes), wait for the correction.
- If the issue cannot be corrected, report it in the Driver app before proceeding.

Do not deliver food you know to be incorrect if the restaurant can fix it in a reasonable time.

---

## Driver Pay and Earnings

Driver pay is calculated per delivery and includes:

- Base delivery fee
- Distance-based pay
- Customer tips (100% passed to driver)
- Bonuses during peak hours or high-demand zones

Earnings are deposited weekly by default. Instant cashout is available for a small fee. For pay disputes, contact Driver Support through the app under **Earnings > Report an Issue**.

---

## Account Issues

If you experience a login issue or account suspension:

1. Try resetting your password through the Driver app login screen.
2. If you believe your account was suspended in error, submit an appeal through **Driver App > Account > Appeal a Decision**.
3. Appeals are typically reviewed within 3 business days.

---

## Driver Safety

Redis Eats takes driver safety seriously. If you ever feel unsafe during a delivery:

- Trust your instincts. You are never required to complete a delivery that makes you feel unsafe.
- Use the **Emergency Assistance** button in the Driver app to alert Redis Eats and optionally contact emergency services.
- All safety-related incidents are reviewed by the Driver Trust and Safety team.

---

## Contact Driver Support

Driver Support is available 24/7 through the Redis Eats Driver app under **Help > Contact Support**.
