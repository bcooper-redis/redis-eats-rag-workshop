# Redis Eats Restaurant Onboarding Guide

## Overview

Welcome to Redis Eats. This guide walks new restaurant partners through the onboarding process, platform setup, and how to manage your presence on Redis Eats.

---

## Step 1: Apply to Join Redis Eats

To become a Redis Eats partner restaurant:

1. Visit the Redis Eats Partner Portal at partner.rediseats.example.com.
2. Complete the restaurant application form with:
   - Restaurant name, address, and cuisine type
   - Owner or manager contact information
   - Business license number
   - Bank account details for payouts
3. Submit your application. You will receive a confirmation email within 1 business day.

Applications are typically reviewed within **3–5 business days**.

---

## Step 2: Onboarding Call

Once approved, a Redis Eats Partner Success representative will schedule a 30-minute onboarding call with you. During this call you will:

- Review your partnership agreement and commission structure
- Set up your restaurant profile on the platform
- Learn how to use the Restaurant Dashboard
- Discuss expected delivery radius and hours

---

## Step 3: Menu Setup

A Redis Eats menu specialist will help you configure your digital menu. You can also manage your menu directly through the Restaurant Dashboard.

Menu setup includes:

- Item names, descriptions, and prices
- Modifiers and customization options (e.g., extra sauce, no onions)
- Item availability by time of day
- Photos (strongly recommended — restaurants with photos see higher order volumes)
- Allergen and dietary flags (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free)

Menus must be reviewed and approved by Redis Eats before going live. This usually takes **1 business day**.

---

## Step 4: Go Live

Before going live, verify:

- Your menu is accurate and complete
- Your restaurant hours are configured correctly
- Your tablet or point-of-sale integration is working
- You have reviewed the order notification process

Redis Eats will notify you when your restaurant is visible to customers.

---

## Managing Your Restaurant on Redis Eats

### Pausing Orders

If your kitchen is overwhelmed or you need to temporarily stop accepting orders:

1. Open the Redis Eats Restaurant Dashboard or tablet app.
2. Tap **Pause Orders**.
3. Select a pause duration (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or custom).

Orders will not be accepted during the pause. Customers will see your restaurant as temporarily unavailable.

**Important:** Pausing orders frequently or for long periods can affect your restaurant's ranking in search results. Use this feature only when necessary.

### Updating Hours

To change your operating hours:

1. Go to **Restaurant Dashboard > Settings > Hours**.
2. Update your hours for the affected day(s).
3. Save changes. Updates take effect immediately.

### Updating Menu Items

Menu changes made through the Dashboard are reviewed automatically. Most changes go live within 30 minutes. Significant changes (new items, price changes above 20%) may require a brief review.

---

## Commissions and Payouts

Redis Eats charges a commission on each order. Your specific commission rate is outlined in your partnership agreement. Payouts are deposited weekly to your registered bank account.

For payout questions or disputes, contact your Partner Success representative or use the **Financial Support** section of the Partner Portal.

For onboarding assistance or ongoing partner support, contact Redis Eats Partner Support at partners@rediseats.example.com or through the Partner Portal.
