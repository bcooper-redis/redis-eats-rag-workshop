# Redis Eats Account and Login Help

## Overview

This document covers common account and login issues for Redis Eats customers, including password resets, account access problems, and how to update your account information.

---

## Creating an Account

To create a Redis Eats account:

1. Download the Redis Eats app from the App Store or Google Play, or visit rediseats.example.com.
2. Tap **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your name, email address, and a password.
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
5. Add a delivery address and payment method to start ordering.

You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account.

---

## Logging In

To log in to your Redis Eats account:

1. Open the app or website.
2. Tap **Log In**.
3. Enter your email address and password.
4. Tap **Sign In**.

If you signed up with Google or Apple, use the corresponding sign-in button instead.

---

## Resetting Your Password

If you have forgotten your password:

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email from Redis Eats.
4. Click the reset link in the email. The link is valid for **30 minutes**.
5. Enter and confirm your new password.
6. Log in with your new password.

If you do not receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If it is still not there, make sure you are using the correct email address for your account.

---

## Account Locked

Your account may be temporarily locked after multiple failed login attempts. If this happens:

- Wait **15 minutes** and try again.
- If you are still unable to log in, use the **Forgot Password?** flow to reset your credentials.
- If the issue persists, contact Redis Eats Support.

---

## Updating Your Account Information

To update your name, email, phone number, or delivery address:

1. Open the Redis Eats app.
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
3. Go to **Account Settings**.
4. Select the field you want to update and make your changes.
5. Save your changes.

Changing your email address will trigger a verification email to your new address. You must verify the new email before the change takes effect.

---

## Updating Payment Methods

To add, remove, or update a payment method:

1. Go to **Account Settings > Payment Methods**.
2. Tap **Add Payment Method** to add a new card or payment option.
3. Tap the three-dot menu next to an existing method to edit or remove it.

You must have at least one valid payment method on file to place orders.

---

## Two-Factor Authentication

Redis Eats supports two-factor authentication (2FA) for added account security. To enable 2FA:

1. Go to **Account Settings > Security**.
2. Tap **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method: SMS or authenticator app.
4. Follow the prompts to complete setup.

Once enabled, you will be asked to verify your identity each time you log in from a new device.

---

## Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete your Redis Eats account:

1. Go to **Account Settings > Privacy**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm your decision.

Account deletion is permanent. All order history, saved addresses, and payment methods will be removed. This action cannot be undone.

---

## Contact Support

For account or login issues that are not resolved by this guide, contact Redis Eats Support through the app or at support@rediseats.example.com.
