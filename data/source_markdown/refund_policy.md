# Redis Eats Refund Policy

## Overview

Redis Eats is committed to ensuring every customer has a great experience. If something goes wrong with your order, our refund policy is here to make it right. This document explains when refunds are available, how to request them, and what to expect.

---

## Eligibility for Refunds

You may be eligible for a full or partial refund in the following situations:

- **Wrong items delivered:** You received items that were not part of your order.
- **Missing items:** One or more items from your order did not arrive.
- **Food quality issues:** Food arrived cold, spoiled, or clearly not prepared as ordered.
- **Order never arrived:** Your order was marked delivered but you did not receive it.
- **Significant delay:** Your order arrived more than 45 minutes past the estimated delivery time.

Refund eligibility is assessed on a case-by-case basis. Redis Eats reserves the right to decline refund requests that do not meet the above criteria or that show patterns of repeated requests.

---

## How to Request a Refund

1. Open the Redis Eats app or website.
2. Navigate to **Order History** and select the affected order.
3. Tap **Report a Problem** and choose the issue that best describes your situation.
4. Provide any additional details or photos if prompted.
5. Submit your request within **48 hours** of the delivery time.

Refund requests submitted more than 48 hours after delivery may not be honored.

---

## Refund Methods

Approved refunds are issued in one of two ways:

- **Redis Eats Credit:** Applied to your account within 1 business day. This is the fastest option.
- **Original Payment Method:** Returned to the card or payment method used for the order. This typically takes 3–5 business days depending on your bank.

You may indicate your preference when submitting your request. Redis Eats Credit is the default for smaller amounts.

---

## Partial Refunds

If only part of your order was affected, you may receive a partial refund for the specific items involved. For example, if one item out of five was missing, the refund will reflect the cost of that item plus applicable taxes and fees proportional to that item.

---

## Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not available for:

- Orders canceled by the customer after the restaurant has begun preparing the food.
- Dissatisfaction with food taste where the item was prepared as described.
- Delivery addresses entered incorrectly by the customer.
- Orders affected by circumstances outside Redis Eats control, such as severe weather events.

---

## Contact Support

If you have questions about a refund or need additional assistance, contact Redis Eats Support through the app or at support@rediseats.example.com.
