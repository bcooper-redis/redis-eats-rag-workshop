# Redis Eats Cancellation Policy

## Overview

We understand that plans change. This document explains when and how you can cancel a Redis Eats order, what fees may apply, and how cancellations affect your refund.

---

## Cancellation Window

Order cancellations are time-sensitive. The ability to cancel depends on where your order is in the preparation process:

| Order Status | Cancellation Available | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Order placed, restaurant not yet confirmed | Yes — full refund | Full refund to original payment |
| Restaurant confirmed, preparation not started | Yes — full refund | Full refund to original payment |
| Restaurant has started preparing the food | Limited — see below | Partial or no refund |
| Order picked up by driver | Not available | No refund |
| Order delivered | Not available | See Refund Policy |

---

## How to Cancel

1. Open the Redis Eats app.
2. Go to **Active Orders**.
3. Select your order and tap **Cancel Order**.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

If the **Cancel Order** option is not available, the order has progressed to a stage where cancellation is no longer possible through the app. Contact Redis Eats Support immediately for assistance.

---

## Cancellations After Preparation Has Started

If a restaurant has already begun preparing your food, cancellations may result in a partial or no refund. This is because the restaurant incurs costs when food preparation begins. In these cases:

- You will see a message indicating the refund amount before confirming the cancellation.
- Redis Eats Credit may be offered as an alternative to a partial refund.

---

## Restaurant-Initiated Cancellations

Occasionally, a restaurant may cancel your order due to:

- Being too busy to fulfill the order in a reasonable time.
- Running out of a key ingredient.
- Technical issues on the restaurant side.

In all cases where a restaurant cancels your order, you will receive a **full refund** to your original payment method within 3–5 business days, or as Redis Eats Credit within 1 business day.

---

## Driver-Related Cancellations

If no driver is available to pick up your order within a reasonable window, Redis Eats may cancel the order on your behalf. You will be notified immediately and receive a full refund.

---

## Repeated Cancellations

Accounts that show a pattern of repeated cancellations after food preparation has started may be subject to a cancellation fee on future orders. Redis Eats will notify you before applying any such fee.

---

## Contact Support

For cancellation questions or assistance with an order that cannot be canceled through the app, contact Redis Eats Support in the app or at support@rediseats.example.com.
